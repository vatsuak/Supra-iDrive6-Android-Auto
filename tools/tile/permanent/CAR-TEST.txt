Later parked-car acceptance tests — record results rather than assuming a pass

1. Confirm exact installed v1.9.91 and original plugin; preserve baseline logs.
   Keep USB logs available; logs may contain phone identifiers, Wi-Fi credentials
   and vehicle location. Redact those before sharing outside this project.
2. With the helper OFF, keep AA connected for 15 minutes, including music/nav use.
   Record every dropout and reconnection. Repeat matched conditions with the tile ON.
   Inspect five-second no-data timeouts; do not attribute them from timing alone.
3. Verify tile Main Menu/Media presence and selection, MENU/BACK/knob/volume,
   camera/PDC handoff, and absence of unwanted screen changes.
4. Disconnect/reconnect Android three times. Tile must hide/show without spawning
   more than one helper. Stop/Restart AA twice. Check helper exit and replacement.
5. Uncheck the tile during AA. It must disappear while AA remains connected.
   Re-enable it and check reappearance. After a helper failure, confirm it does
   not relaunch repeatedly; inspect the log/lock before retrying.
6. Stop AA. Connect the iPhone with factory CarPlay. Verify normal CarPlay entry,
   audio and controls. Disconnect iPhone, start AA and reconnect Android. Repeat
   in the reverse order. Test simultaneous phone availability separately; this
   tile does not arbitrate which projection connection owns the screen.
7. Reboot with AA automatic start OFF and the tile setting OFF, then ON. Confirm
   the saved setting is accurate and AA still starts manually. Verify rollback
   restores the exact original plugin and retains existing AA settings.
8. Only after the above pass, test the existing Start automatically setting ON:
   Android first, iPhone first, no phone, and subsequent phone reconnects. Confirm
   AA bootloop protection remains effective and no helper remains after AA stops.

Release gate: longer paired comparisons without unexplained regression, native UI
and launch compatibility, clean stop/restart/disable, CarPlay/camera behavior,
verified rollback and reboot tests. Until then this stays an experimental candidate.
